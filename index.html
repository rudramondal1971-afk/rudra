📘 Quick User Guide

  1. Upload your Bank Statement and Tally Export (.xlsx) in Step 1.
  2. Adjust date tolerance and narration similarity in Step 2 if needed.
  3. Click Run Reconciliation in Step 3.
  4. Review Match Summary, Mismatch Details, and Narration Mismatch sections.
  5. Use Manual Matching for anything the engine couldn't pair automatically.
  6. Export your final Excel/CSV report or print a client-ready statement.
